Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Introduction

Sarvahit Global Foundation respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect and handle information when you visit our website, contact us, volunteer with us or support our initiatives.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us, submit a volunteer application, make an enquiry, subscribe to our updates or interact with our website.

This information may include your name, email address, phone number, location and any other information you choose to provide through our forms or communication channels.

How We Use Your Information

The information you provide may be used to respond to your enquiries, communicate with you, process volunteer requests, provide information about our initiatives and improve our website and services.

Where you have chosen to receive updates from us, we may use your contact information to share relevant news, activities and opportunities related to Sarvahit Global Foundation.

Donations & Payments

Online donations are processed securely by our payment gateway partner, Razorpay. Your card, UPI and net banking details are entered on Razorpay's secure checkout and are not stored by Sarvahit Global Foundation. We receive only the details needed to record your donation, such as your name, email address, phone number, donation amount and the transaction ID.

To issue an 80G tax receipt, we may ask for your PAN and address. As required under the Income Tax Act, 1961, details of donations for which 80G receipts are issued are reported to the Income Tax Department.

Razorpay processes payment information under its own privacy policy. For refunds, please see our Refund Policy.

Data Protection & Security

Sarvahit Global Foundation takes reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy. Donation and receipt records are kept for the period required by applicable tax and accounting laws.

Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, social media platforms or external services. These websites operate independently and have their own privacy policies. Sarvahit Global Foundation is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand visitor interactions and provide a better browsing experience. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, although some website features may not work as intended.

Children's Privacy

Sarvahit Global Foundation is committed to protecting children's privacy. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through our website without appropriate consent or lawful basis.

Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding the personal information we hold about you, including requesting access, correction or deletion of your information or withdrawing consent where applicable.

Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page with the revised effective date.
