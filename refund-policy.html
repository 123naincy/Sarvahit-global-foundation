Refund Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Overview

Sarvahit Global Foundation is a Section 8 company registered in India. Donations made to the foundation are voluntary contributions that support our charitable work in education, healthcare, nutrition, environment and community welfare.

Because donations are put to use for our programmes soon after they are received, donations are generally non-refundable. We do, however, refund donations in the limited cases listed below.

When a Refund Can Be Requested

We will consider a refund if:

  • the same donation was charged more than once (duplicate transaction);
  • an incorrect amount was donated because of a typing or technical error;
  • money was debited from your account but the payment failed or the donation was not recorded; or
  • the payment was made from your account without your authorisation.

For an unauthorised payment, please also inform your bank or card issuer immediately.

How to Request a Refund

Email sarvahitglobalfoundation@gmail.com within 7 days of the date of donation with the following details:

  • your full name, and the email address and phone number used for the donation;
  • the date and amount of the donation;
  • the payment or transaction ID shown in your payment confirmation; and
  • the reason for the refund request.

Requests received after 7 days, or without enough information to identify the payment, may not be eligible for a refund.

Refund Processing

We will review your request and reply by email. If the refund is approved, the amount will be returned to the original payment method (card, UPI, net banking or wallet) through our payment gateway within 5–7 working days of approval.

The time taken for the amount to appear in your account after it has been processed depends on your bank or payment provider. Refunds are not paid in cash or to a different account.

Failed Transactions

If money was debited but the payment did not complete, the amount is normally reversed to your account automatically by your bank or payment provider. If you have not received it within 7 working days, please contact us with the transaction details and we will help you follow up.

Cancellation of Recurring Donations

If you have set up a recurring donation, you can cancel future payments at any time by emailing sarvahitglobalfoundation@gmail.com. Cancellation stops payments from the next due date onwards. Payments that have already been made are covered by the refund rules on this page.

80G Tax Receipts

Donations to Sarvahit Global Foundation are eligible for tax deduction under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961, subject to the conditions of the Act. If a donation is refunded, any 80G receipt issued for that donation is cancelled and the refunded amount cannot be claimed as a deduction.
