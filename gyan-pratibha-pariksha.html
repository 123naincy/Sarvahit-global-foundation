11 October 2026 Garhi Village, Hansi, Haryana

Gyan Pratibha Pariksha 2026: Winners Honoured by Sarvhit Global Foundation

Sarvhit Global Foundation organised the prize distribution ceremony for the Gyan Pratibha Pariksha (Knowledge Talent Examination) on 11 October 2026 at the girls' library managed by the Foundation at Basanti Bhawan, Garhi village, Hansi, Haryana.

The examination was conducted on 27 September 2026 and received an enthusiastic response, with more than 100 female students participating. The initiative aimed to encourage students, recognise their achievements and motivate them to continue their educational journey.

All participants received gifts as a token of encouragement. Six winning students were honoured with prizes including bicycles, watches, school bags, diaries and pens.

The ceremony concluded successfully in the gracious presence of Gram Panchayat members, respected village residents and local businesspersons Mukesh and Mahesh. The event reflected the Foundation's commitment to promoting education and supporting young learners.
