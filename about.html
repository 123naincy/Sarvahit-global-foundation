About Us

Sarvahit Global Foundation
Small Steps. Lasting Impact.

Driven by Purpose, Inspired by Humanity

Sarvahit Global Foundation is committed to uplifting communities through education, healthcare, environment, and social welfare. We believe every individual deserves equal opportunities to learn, grow, and live with dignity.







Education is at the heart of our mission, with a special focus on creating free and accessible libraries. We aim to provide children and young learners with spaces where knowledge inspires dreams and builds brighter futures.



Through healthcare, nutrition, sanitation, sports, empowerment, and awareness initiatives, we strive to create meaningful grassroots impact. Our purpose is simple—to serve humanity and build stronger, healthier, and empowered communities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Sarvahit Global Foundation is committed to creating positive social impact through education, healthcare, environment, nutrition, empowerment and community welfare initiatives. Our goal is to create opportunities for people to learn, grow and live with dignity.

Our initiatives focus on education, healthcare, nutrition, environmental awareness, child welfare, women empowerment, sports, sanitation, awareness and community development. We aim to address meaningful needs at the grassroots level.

You can support our mission by contributing to our initiatives, volunteering your time and skills, supporting educational and community programs, or helping spread awareness about our work. Every effort can contribute towards creating positive change.

Yes. Individuals who want to contribute their time, skills or experience towards social initiatives can connect with Sarvahit Global Foundation and explore suitable volunteering opportunities. Together, we can work towards stronger and more empowered communities.

You can contact Sarvahit Global Foundation through our contact page, phone number or official social media channels. Our team will be happy to guide you regarding volunteering, collaboration and our initiatives.

Together We Can Build a Better Tomorrow

Sarvahit Global Foundation is committed to creating positive and lasting change in communities across India. Through education, healthcare, environmental initiatives and social welfare, we work to create opportunities for people to learn, grow and live with dignity.

  • Making quality education and learning resources accessible to every child.
  • Supporting healthier and stronger communities through healthcare, nutrition and awareness.
  • Empowering individuals and families with opportunities for a better future.
  • Build an India where every individual has the opportunity to learn, grow and live with dignity.
  • Create stronger, healthier and more sustainable communities.
  • Inspire people to come together and contribute towards social change.
  • Compassion — serving people with empathy and respect.
  • Inclusion — creating opportunities for everyone without discrimination.
  • Integrity — working with honesty, responsibility and transparency.
