Return Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

No Products Sold

Sarvahit Global Foundation is a Section 8 company registered in India that works for social welfare. Payments made on this website are donations to support our charitable work. They are not purchases of goods or services.

Because we do not sell or ship any physical products, there are no items to return or exchange, and no shipping or delivery charges apply.

Donation Receipts

Donation receipts and 80G tax receipts are documents that confirm your contribution. They are not products and cannot be returned. If any details on your receipt are incorrect, please contact us and we will help correct them.

Refunds

If you made a donation by mistake, for example a duplicate payment or a wrong amount, please see our Refund Policy for the eligible cases and how to request a refund within 7 days.
